## Executive Summary

TARCMS is a digital management and public information platform designed for Tepi Agricultural Research Center (TARC). The project aims to create a centralized system that supports institutional operations, public communication, and research information management. The system combines a public-facing website with an administrative dashboard to help authorized users manage and update information related to departments, staff, research programs, projects, publications, events, gallery materials, and contact messages.

The internship was important because agricultural research institutions often face challenges related to scattered information, weak institutional visibility, and the difficulty of maintaining data in a structured way. TARCMS addresses these issues by creating a single digital platform that improves accessibility, organization, and communication. The project is not only a website; it is a structured management system designed to make important institutional information easier to store, maintain, and share.

During this internship, I worked on system requirement analysis, project design, interface development, backend integration, testing, and report documentation. The project helped me connect theoretical concepts from my coursework to real-world software development. It also gave me practical experience in designing a useful system for a real institution, which is the core value of the internship.

# 3. How and Why the Project Was Selected and Worked Out

## 3.1 Project Title and Short Summary

### Project Title
TARCMS — Tepi Agricultural Research Center Management System

### Short Summary
The project is a digital management and communication system for Tepi Agricultural Research Center. It provides a public portal for institutional information and an admin dashboard for authorized staff to manage important records such as departments, research programs, projects, publications, events, and contact messages.

------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.2 Problem Statement and Justification

Agricultural research institutions generate large amounts of valuable information, including project records, publication outputs, staff details, and institutional announcements. In many cases, this information is scattered across manual documents or static webpages, which makes it difficult to maintain and access. This creates challenges for communication, transparency, and institutional visibility.

TARCMS addresses this problem by creating a digital platform that centralizes information and allows authorized users to maintain it in a structured way. The project is justified because TARC needs a professional and manageable system that supports both its public communication and administrative work.

------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.3 Objective of the Project

The general objective of the project is to design and implement a digital system that supports the management and presentation of institutional information at TARC. The specific objectives are:

- To provide a public website for TARC
- To create an administrative dashboard for authorized users
- To organize departments, staff, and research programs
- To manage projects and publications systematically
- To support news, events, and gallery content
- To improve communication and institutional visibility
- To provide a foundation for future digital expansion

------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.4 Methodology

The project followed a structured methodology that included the following steps:

1. Requirement analysis and problem definition
2. Review of institutional needs and system documents
3. System design and architecture planning
4. Frontend and backend implementation
5. Database design and integration
6. Testing and validation
7. Documentation and final report preparation

This approach ensured that the system was planned, tested, and documented in a stable and professional manner.

------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.5 Literature Review

The literature on institutional information systems shows that digital platforms are increasingly important for organizations that need to manage large amounts of information and communicate with multiple stakeholders. Research institutions benefit from digital systems because they can improve information transparency, strengthen communications, and make research outputs easier to access.

A web-based management system helps institutions provide a professional online presence while also ensuring that authorized users can maintain content efficiently. In the context of agricultural research centers, this type of system is essential for effective communication, institutional branding, and efficient management of records.

------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.6 Result and Discussion

The project produced a working digital system that supports a large part of TARC’s operational and public communication needs. The public portal allows visitors to access institutional information, while the dashboard allows staff to manage critical content efficiently. This makes the system more useful than a standard static website because it supports both information display and internal data management.

The result is beneficial because it supports a digital transformation pathway for the institution. The system can help TARC improve its communication with stakeholders, update public information more easily, and maintain organized records of research activities and staff information.
