About TARC

Cultivating the Future of Ethiopian Agriculture

Nestled in the biodiverse southwest, the Tepi Agricultural Research Center serves as a vital hub for scientific inquiry and agricultural innovation. Our mission is to transform traditional farming methodologies through rigorous research, ensuring food security and economic prosperity for the region.

We specialize in highland crops, focusing on disease resistance, climate adaptability, and maximizing yield potential while preserving the delicate ecological balance of our surroundings.
