# TARC Open Questions & Institutional Decisions Required

> **Document Purpose**: Dedicated checklist of institutional data, policies, and procedural approvals required from Tepi Agricultural Research Center leadership prior to final production cutover.  
> **Rule**: Do not invent official data; seed datasets represent realistic simulations until officially supplied by the Center.

---

## 1. Institutional Identity & Branding
- [ ] **Official Center Name & Affiliation**: Confirm exact official wording in English and Amharic (e.g., *Tepi Agricultural Research Center under Ethiopian Institute of Agricultural Research (EIAR)* or *South Agricultural Research Institute (SARI)*).
- [ ] **Official Logo & Color Palette**: High-resolution vector/PNG logo and official institutional primary/secondary colors.
- [ ] **Leadership Profile**: Official photo, biography, and welcome message from the Center Director.
- [ ] **Official Contact Channels**: Official public telephone numbers, general inquiry email, postal address (P.O. Box), and verified GPS coordinates for map embedding.

---

## 2. Organizational Structure & Personnel
- [ ] **Confirmed Department List**: Final list of official research and support departments (e.g., Spices Research, Coffee Research, Forestry/Agroforestry, Crop Protection, Soil & Water, Agricultural Economics).
- [ ] **Staff Roster & Privacy Approvals**:
  - Full list of research and administrative personnel.
  - Confirmation of which staff contact details (email, phone) are approved for public visibility versus restricted to internal management.
- [ ] **Department Heads**: Designated heads of each department for organizational chart rendering.

---

## 3. Research Domain & Publications Data
- [ ] **Active Research Programs**: Official list of established programs, codes, and mandate descriptions.
- [ ] **Current Active Projects**: Official inventory of running research trials, funding partners, and principal investigators.
- [ ] **Scientific Publications & Bulletins**: Catalog of historical and recent publications, research bulletins, production manuals, and variety release papers with PDF attachments.

---

## 4. Operational Resources & Fleet Policies
- [ ] **Vehicle Fleet Inventory**: Accurate list of official vehicles (registration plate numbers, make/model, year, vehicle type, and regular drivers).
- [ ] **Vehicle Requisition Policy**:
  - Who has authority to approve vehicle dispatch requests (e.g., Department Head, Transport Officer, Center Director)?
  - Are vehicle maintenance logs and mileage tracking required for daily operations?

---

## 5. Security & Authorization Boundaries
- [ ] **Initial System Administrators**: Designation of email addresses for initial `SUPER_ADMIN` and `ADMIN` accounts.
- [ ] **Researcher Access Scope**: Confirmation of whether researchers can edit any project within their department or strictly projects where they are designated Principal Investigator / Co-Researcher.
- [ ] **Content Moderation Workflow**: Confirmation of whether news articles, events, and publications require Director/Admin approval before appearing on the public portal or can be published directly by authorized editors.
