Pioneering Agricultural Excellence in the Sheka Highlands

Tepi Agricultural Research Center (TARC) is committed to advancing sustainable farming practices and preserving the rich ecological heritage of Southwest Ethiopia.

eco

Our Mission

To generate, improve, and promote appropriate agricultural technologies that ensure food security, increase productivity, and conserve natural resources in the unique agro-ecological zones of the Sheka region and beyond.

visibility

Our Vision

To be a leading center of excellence in agricultural research, driving sustainable development and transforming the livelihoods of farming communities in Southwest Ethiopia by 2030.

Institutional Objectives

science

Applied Research

Conducting rigorous, context-specific research on crops, livestock, and natural resource management tailored to highland ecosystems.

handshake

Technology Transfer

Facilitating the rapid dissemination and adoption of proven agricultural innovations among local farmers and stakeholders.

landscape

Ecological Stewardship

Promoting farming practices that maintain soil health, conserve water, and protect the biodiversity of the Sheka biosphere.

Message from the Director

Welcome to the Tepi Agricultural Research Center. Our institution stands at the forefront of agricultural innovation in one of Ethiopia's most ecologically significant regions.

The challenges of modern agriculture require a delicate balance between increasing yield to ensure food security and preserving the delicate environmental equilibrium of the Sheka highlands. Our dedicated team of scientists and researchers work tirelessly to develop resilient, high-yielding crop varieties and sustainable farming methodologies.

Through collaborative partnerships with local communities, government bodies, and international research organizations, we are transforming research data into actionable practices that empower farmers and protect our natural heritage for future generations.

Dr. Alemayehu Tadesse

Center Director, TARC
