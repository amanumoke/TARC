1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
TARCMS is a proposed digital management and public information platform for the Tepi Agricultural Research
Center. The project is intended to bring important institutional information and selected management activities into
one organized, accessible and maintainable system.
The system serves two major purposes. First, it provides the public with reliable information about the Center, its
research activities, people, publications, news, events and other approved institutional content. Second, it provides
authorized Center personnel with a controlled place to maintain information and manage selected records.
The idea is therefore broader than creating a normal website. TARCMS is intended to become a digital institutional
platform that improves how information is presented, maintained, discovered and shared.
2. PROJECT NAME
TARCMS stands for Tepi Agricultural Research Center Management System.
The name reflects the wider purpose of the project: the system is not limited to displaying information. It combines a
public-facing institutional presence with management capabilities for selected Center information and activities.
3. THE PROBLEM THE PROJECT ADDRESSES
Agricultural research institutions generate valuable information: research programs, projects, publications, staff
expertise, events, announcements, institutional history and other resources. When information is difficult to find,
outdated, scattered across different channels or dependent on manual communication, its value is reduced.
TARCMS addresses this challenge by proposing one organized digital platform where approved information can be
presented to the public and maintained by authorized users.
The system is also intended to reduce dependence on static pages that require technical changes whenever
institutional information needs updating. With a management dashboard, authorized personnel can maintain content
more systematically.
4. IMPORTANCE TO THE INSTITUTE
• Institutional visibility: presents the Center, its research activities and achievements through a professional digital
presence.
• Information organization: brings important institutional information into a structured system.
• Content maintenance: lets authorized personnel update approved information without rebuilding the whole
website.
• Research visibility: makes research programs, projects and publications easier to discover.
• Staff visibility: provides a structured way to present approved staff information and expertise.
• Communication: provides a clear channel for news, events and contact messages.
• Institutional memory: creates an organized digital record of selected content.
• Operational support: selected modules such as vehicles can organize internal records.
• Decision support: dashboard summaries can help authorized personnel see current information quickly.
• Future growth: the platform can later be expanded when new needs are identified.
5. IMPORTANCE TO RESEARCHERS

• Publications can be organized so research outputs are easier to find.

9. WHO WILL USE TARCMS?
Public visitors: people who want information about the Center, research, publications, news, events, gallery and
contact details.
Center management: authorized personnel who need management information and selected administrative
functions.
System administrators: authorized personnel responsible for maintaining system content and settings.
Researchers and staff: information consumers and, where authorized, contributors to institutional content.
Students, partners and community members: users who primarily consume approved public information.
Note: exact internal responsibilities and permissions should be confirmed by the Center.
10. PUBLIC WEBSITE — WHAT PEOPLE WILL SEE
The public side is the Center's digital front door. It should be welcoming, professional, accessible and focused on
useful institutional information.
Home: Introduces the Center, highlights important research/content and directs visitors to key information.
About: Explains approved background, mission, vision, objectives and institutional identity.
Director: Presents an approved director/leadership message and profile.
Departments: Shows departments or organizational units and their descriptions.
Research Programs: Presents research areas/programs and their purpose.
Projects: Presents approved research projects and their information.
Publications: Provides an organized presentation of research publications.
News: Communicates institutional announcements and important updates.
Events: Presents upcoming and past events where appropriate.
Gallery: Displays approved photographs and media.
Contact: Provides official contact information and a visitor message form.
11. MANAGEMENT SIDE — WHAT THE CENTER CAN MANAGE
The management side is for authorized users. It is where selected information shown on the public website can be
maintained and selected internal records can be organized.
Dashboard: Provides an overview of important information, summaries and quick actions.
Staff: Maintains approved staff records and related information.
Departments: Maintains department information.
Research Programs: Maintains research program information.
Projects: Maintains research project records.
Publications: Maintains publication records and related files where approved.
News: Creates and manages institutional news.
Events: Creates and manages events.
Gallery: Manages approved images and media.
Vehicles: Maintains selected vehicle/resource information for internal management.
Messages: Allows authorized users to review contact messages.

Settings: Manages selected system/institution configuration.

12. STAFF MANAGEMENT
Staff information is important because people are central to research. The proposed staff module can organize
approved information such as name, position, department, professional description, areas of expertise and
photograph.
Exact fields should be approved by the Center. Sensitive personal information should not be collected simply because it is
technically possible.
13. RESEARCH PROGRAM MANAGEMENT
The research program module provides a structured way to describe the Center's research areas. Each program can
have a title, description, objectives, responsible department/team and links to related projects where appropriate.
14. RESEARCH PROJECT MANAGEMENT
Projects represent specific research activities. The system can organize approved project information such as title,
description, research program, responsible team, dates, status and related outputs.
15. PUBLICATION MANAGEMENT
Publications are major research outputs. TARCMS can provide a structured publication catalog containing information
such as title, authors, year, publication type, description/abstract, related project and optional document or external
reference.

16. NEWS AND EVENTS
News provides a channel for announcements, achievements, institutional updates and other approved
communication. Events can communicate meetings, workshops, conferences, field activities, training and other
approved activities.
17. GALLERY
The gallery provides a visual record of approved Center activities, facilities, research work, events and other
institutional moments. Images should be uploaded with appropriate permission and descriptive information.
18. VEHICLE MANAGEMENT
The proposed vehicle module is an internal management feature. It can organize basic vehicle/resource records such
as registration identifier, vehicle type, assigned department/responsibility, status and other fields approved by the
Center.
The project should not invent vehicle inventories. Actual vehicles, fields and management procedures must come from the Center.
19. CONTACT AND MESSAGE MANAGEMENT
The contact module connects the public website with the institution. Visitors can submit a message through an
approved contact form, while authorized personnel can review and manage incoming messages.
20. INFORMATION FLOW
Institution provides/approves information → authorized user enters/updates it → system stores it → approved content is
published → public visitor views it.
Visitor opens Contact → submits message → system validates/stores message → authorized user reviews it → Center
responds through its normal process.

25. PRIVACY AND RESPONSIBLE INFORMATION
MANAGEMENT
Because TARCMS may contain staff, operational and communication information, the Center should decide what is
appropriate to store and display. Personal or sensitive information should be minimized, protected and only exposed
to authorized users.
Photographs, documents and other media should be used with appropriate institutional permission. Public information
should be separated from internal information.
26. SUCCESS MEASURES
• A visitor can understand what the Center does within a few minutes.
• A visitor can find research programs, projects and publications easily.
• Important announcements and events can be published and maintained.
• Authorized staff can update approved content without changing source code.
• Contact messages can be received and reviewed.
• Information is organized consistently.
• Access to internal functions is restricted.
• The Center can maintain the platform after the internship with appropriate training and documentation.
27. PROJECT LIMITATIONS
TARCMS should not be presented as replacing every existing administrative system at the Center. Its initial scope is
the public institutional platform plus selected management functions. Additional systems such as finance, laboratory,
HR, procurement or inventory should only be added after requirements are properly gathered.
28. CONCLUSION
TARCMS is proposed as a digital platform that can strengthen the Tepi Agricultural Research Center's public
communication, research visibility, information organization and selected internal management activities.
For the public, its value is easier access to useful institutional information. For researchers and students, its value is
improved discovery of research programs, projects and publications. For the Center, its value is a more organized
and maintainable digital presence combined with selected management capabilities.
The most important principle is that the system should be built around the Center's real needs. Official Center information,
workflows, permissions and content must therefore be confirmed by the institution before the system is treated as an operational
production system.
