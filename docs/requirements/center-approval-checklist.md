# TARCMS Center Approval Checklist

## Document Purpose
This document lists the official institutional information and policy decisions still required from Tepi Agricultural Research Center (TARC) leadership before final production sign-off of the TARCMS platform.

> This checklist is based on the project requirements and the formal open-questions document. All technical system work is already in place; the remaining items are institutional approvals and official data needed from the Center.

---

## 1. Institutional Identity & Branding

### 1.1 Official Center Identity
- [ ] Official English name of the center
- [ ] Official Amharic name of the center
- [ ] Official affiliation with EIAR / relevant institute
- [ ] Official logo file (PNG/SVG/PDF)
- [ ] Official institutional color palette
- [ ] Official center motto or tagline, if available

### 1.2 Leadership & Communications
- [ ] Director full name and title
- [ ] Director photo
- [ ] Director biography
- [ ] Director welcome message
- [ ] Official center email address
- [ ] Official phone numbers
- [ ] Postal address / P.O. Box
- [ ] Physical address and GPS coordinates
- [ ] Map embed coordinates

## 7. Final Sign-Off Needed

Before going live in production, the Center should confirm the following:

- [ ] Official institutional identity is approved
- [ ] Department and staff data is approved
- [ ] Research program and publications data is approved
- [ ] Vehicle and operational policy is approved
- [ ] Admin and researcher access policy is approved
- [ ] Public portal content is approved
- [ ] Final legal and operational sign-off is granted

## 8. Approval Message to the Center

Dear TARC Leadership,

The TARCMS system has been developed based on the concept and requirements of the project. The technical implementation is complete and the platform is ready for validation. However, some official institutional data and policy decisions are still required from the Center before final production deployment.

Please review and provide the list of official information requested above so that the system can be finalized with accurate institutional branding, staff records, research data, and governance rules.

Thank you for your support and guidance.

Sincerely,
TARCMS Development Team

## 9. Summary
The remaining requirements are not mainly software development gaps. They are official approvals and real institutional data that must be provided by the Center before final launch.

Once the Center supplies the information above, the system can be finalized, populated, and deployed with actual institutional data.
